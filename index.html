No Reels ✋

Your Instagram chats and stories, without reels.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

No Reels collects no data. There is no No Reels account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your Instagram login

You log in on Instagram's own website inside the app. Your password, messages, photos and stories go directly between your phone and Instagram, and Instagram's own privacy policy applies to them. No Reels never sends them anywhere else.

What stays on your phone

Only on your phone: your reels-dodged counter, your settings, and a copy of your chat list so the home screen opens fast. The chat list copy is deleted when you log out, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Camera, microphone and photos

Used only when you choose to post a story or send a photo or voice message, and only through Instagram's site.

Children

No Reels is for people who can use Instagram under Instagram's own rules (13 and older).

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the change applies.

Support

Chats don't load

Check your connection, then pull down on the home screen. If you were logged out, log in again on Instagram's page in the app.

Message alerts

Open the app, tap the counter at the top of home, then "Set up instant message alerts" for the step-by-step guide.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub.
